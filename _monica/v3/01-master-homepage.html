Have Questions?

FAQ

Do you charge a planning fee?
No. My commission comes from the supplier, not from you. You pay the same (or better) price than booking online — and you get a real human in your corner the entire trip.
What kinds of trips do you book?
All-inclusive resorts, private villas, cruises, honeymoons, destination weddings, family escapes, corporate retreats, bucket-list adventures, group trips, and personal getaways. If it's travel-related, ask — I can probably build it.
How fast can you put a proposal together?
For most trips, 24–72 hours after I have your details. Complex multi-stop, group, or destination-wedding itineraries can take a week. I'll set expectations the moment you submit.
What if I just want flights or a hotel?
Single-night hotel bookings or one-way flights aren't a great use of either of our time — but for full vacations, multi-night resorts, cruises, and packages, I'm absolutely your person.
How does the $50 trip credit work?
If you joined the email list, you'll receive a code (LUXURY50) to apply to your first booking inquiry. The credit is applied to my custom-itinerary services — one per household, valid 90 days from issue.
Do I need travel-industry experience to become an advisor?
No. The training platform walks you through every booking system, supplier portal, and marketing tool — step by step. Most new advisors take their first booking within 30 days.
How much does it cost to get started?
There's a small startup investment for your host-agency credentials, training platform access, and supplier portals. We'll walk through the exact numbers on your discovery call — there are no surprises and no upsells.
How quickly can I start earning?
As soon as you complete onboarding (typically 5–7 days). Most advisors take their first booking within 30 days — usually a friend, family member, or their own next trip.
Is this a pyramid scheme or MLM?
No. You earn commission directly from supplier bookings — flights, hotels, cruises, packages. There's no recruiting requirement, no minimum bookings, no monthly quota. You earn from travel transactions, period.
What support do I get from Monica directly?
1-on-1 onboarding, group mentorship calls, a private operator community, personal branding guidance, and direct access for booking questions. You're not handed a binder and forgotten.
