Questions, answered

Frequently Asked.

Do I need experience to become a travel agent?

None at all. Most of our agents start with zero industry experience. Our training platform walks you through everything — booking systems, supplier relationships, marketing, the works. If you can use a phone, you can do this.

How much can I earn as an Independent Travel Agent?

Income is uncapped. Some agents cover their own travel costs. Some replace their full-time income. Some build six-figure agencies. The pace and ceiling depend on you. We give you the platform — you bring the effort.

Is this MLM or pyramid-related?

No. You're partnered with a licensed host travel agency. You earn commission on real bookings — flights, hotels, cruises, weddings. You're a real travel professional, not selling perfumes to your friends. (You can also build a team if you want a second income stream — but it's optional.)

If I just want to book travel, how does that work?

Tell us where you want to go (or what kind of trip you're dreaming about). We design it, book it, and handle the details. You get insider access to deals and properties most travelers never see — and a real human concierge through the whole trip.

How quickly can I start?

Same week. Once you're enrolled, you get instant access to the training and booking platform. Most new agents have their first booking within their first 30 days.

Where is Callis Lux Travel based?

We're based in the Pearland/Houston, TX area, but we serve travelers and recruit agents nationwide. Everything is run online — calls, training, support — so location doesn't matter.
